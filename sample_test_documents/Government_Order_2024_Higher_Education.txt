GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
MINISTRY OF EDUCATION & HUMAN RESOURCE DEVELOPMENT
DEPARTMENT OF HIGHER EDUCATION

GOVERNMENT ORDER NO. HE-2024/789
Dated: 15th June 2024

SUBJECT: HIGHER EDUCATION SCHOLARSHIP AND FEE REIMBURSEMENT SCHEME 2024

1. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR SCHOLARSHIP GRANTS
1.1 Minimum Student Age Requirement: Students must be at least 18 years of age at the time of application to qualify for higher education grants.
1.2 Family Income Threshold: Annual family income must be below ₹2,50,000 for full fee reimbursement and financial support.
1.3 Mandatory Verification Documents: Applicants must submit a valid Aadhaar Card and Income Certificate issued by a Tehsildar officer.

2. APPLICATION DEADLINE
2.1 Last Date for Online Submission: The final deadline for submitting scholarship applications shall be 31st October 2024.
