GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL JUSTICE & STUDENT WELFARE

GOVERNMENT POLICY NO. SJSW-2025/102
Dated: 10th January 2025

SUBJECT: STATE STUDENT WELFARE SCHOLARSHIP POLICY 2025

1. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR SCHOLARSHIP FUNDING
1.1 Minimum Student Age Requirement: Students must be 21 years or older to receive state scholarship funding under this policy.
1.2 Family Income Threshold: Annual family income must be below ₹3,00,000 for financial assistance.
1.3 Mandatory Verification Documents: Applicants must submit a valid Ration Card and Income Certificate.

2. APPLICATION DEADLINE
2.1 Last Date for Online Submission: The final deadline for submitting scholarship applications is 30th November 2025.
