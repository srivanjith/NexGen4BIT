GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
MINISTRY OF EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT OF HIGHER EDUCATION

GOVERNMENT CIRCULAR NO. HE-CIRCULAR-2025/05
Dated: 1st March 2025

SUBJECT: CLARIFICATION REGARDING STUDENT AGE ELIGIBILITY EXCEPTIONS

1. AGE EXCEPTION PROVISIONS
1.1 Conditional Eligibility: Students aged 18 to 20 years may apply for state scholarship funding provided that they are enrolled in an NAAC Accredited Grade A+ Institution.
1.2 Special Exceptions: Applicants under 18 years are exempted if enrolled in a National Institute of Technology (NIT) or Indian Institute of Technology (IIT).
